    About TruPick
    About

    Why we built TruPick

    Every day, thousands of new products go on sale with zero reviews, and fake or paid reviews make the rest hard to trust. We wanted a simple way to answer one question before checkout: what will this probably be like?

    Our approach

    Learn from look-alikes

    Products of the same kind tend to share the same strengths and problems. We match yours to the closest reviewed ones.

    Listen to real buyers

    We read hundreds of thousands of real buyer reviews and pull out what people actually praise and complain about.

    Be upfront about certainty

    Every result says how close the match is. When we don’t have enough data, we say so instead of guessing.

    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. There’s no sign-up and no limit.

    How accurate is it?

    It shows what usually happens with similar products, not a guarantee for this exact item. Each result shows a confidence level; treat a weak match as a rough guide.

    Where does the data come from?

    Public review datasets: Amazon Reviews 2023 from UC San Diego’s McAuley Lab, and a women’s clothing reviews dataset from Kaggle. We use only the reviews, never personal data.

    Does it work for Indian brands?

    Partly. Most reference products are sold on Amazon US, so the comparison is by product type rather than brand. A boAt earbud is compared with similar earbuds.

    Do you store my searches?

    Your recent searches stay in your own browser. We only save a search if you click Yes or No on “Helpful?”, and only to improve results.
